## Three acquisition channels

```
Ben → Existing network → Founding 10
Ben → Direct prospecting (RE first) → Founding 10
Ben → Founding Resellers → Their network → Founding 10   (Ben remains the closer)
```

After the first 10 are operating:

```
Customers + Partners + Case studies + Referrals → Founding 100
```

Do **not** try to acquire everyone through paid advertising from day one.

### 1. Existing network — first wave (highest priority)

Years of relationships: business owners, real estate, property, finance, trades/services, accommodation, technology, marketing, sales, former clients, suppliers, professional contacts.

**Objective is not to sell everyone immediately.** The conversation is:

> I’m launching something I’ve been building for a long time and I’m looking for the first 10 businesses to build it with.

Not: “Would you like to buy my software?”

Founding 10 is the legitimate reason to contact people personally. Goal of the first touch: **book a conversation**.

### 2. Direct prospecting — Tuesday (real estate first)

Strongest product-market credibility. Target boutique and independent agencies, sales teams, agency principals. Use the four RE frameworks as the content hook, not generic SaaS copy.

### 3. Founding Resellers — Wednesday (qualified partners, not affiliates)

Do **not** position this as a generic affiliate programme. Recruit people who can **open doors**.

## Founding Customer vs Founding Reseller

These are **different roles**. Do not collapse them.

| Role | Who | What they do | What they get |
|------|-----|--------------|---------------|
| **Founding Customer** | A business that primarily **uses** DigitalGate | Applies, consults, onboards, operates | Cohort acquisition discount (24 months) + the referral *entitlement* in their offer |
| **Founding Reseller** | A person/business that **actively introduces** DigitalGate | Identifies and introduces suitable businesses | Partner status, attribution, assets, commission on qualifying referred subscriptions |

A Founding 10 customer’s offer may include Founding Reseller **referral terms**. That does **not** make every customer an active partner.

**Founding Reseller (partner channel):**

- Application / approval based — not open signup
- Does **not** need to be a software person; sales / BD people are ideal
- Does **not** get a login and a brief to sell a complex Business Operating Platform
- They **identify and introduce**; **Ben handles discovery, demonstration, and closing** until the sales process is proven
- Then train partners to do more

Do **not** advertise for resellers yet. Do **not** let everyone call themselves a reseller.

### Who qualifies (Tier 1)

People who already have trusted commercial relationships with Australian SME owners:

Business development managers · account managers · sales consultants · real estate BDMs · commercial property agents · business brokers · finance brokers · IT consultants · MSPs · web developers · marketing consultants · business consultants · telecoms salespeople · POS/payment consultants · commercial insurance brokers · accountants with strong SME relationships

They already have conversations DigitalGate belongs in: how’s the business going, what systems, how do you generate leads, what’s happening with the website, how do you manage customers.

A good partner can introduce the **whole platform** (Platform + Apps + implementation + support), not a $99 software referral.

### Qualification bar

Ideal partner:

- Established network of Australian businesses
- Regularly speaks with owners / decision-makers
- Genuine sales / BD capability
- Understands DigitalGate’s value and can identify suitable prospects
- Willing to make introductions
- Maintains professional standards
- Does not misrepresent capabilities
- Does not promise features, pricing, or delivery DigitalGate has not confirmed

### First partners (Ben recruits)

1. List ~30 people from the career network who have sales/business relationships.
2. Rank: **A** — trust to represent DigitalGate · **B** — potential · **C** — introduction source only.
3. Personally approach the **A list**.
4. Aim for **up to 10 selected Founding Resellers** (Founding 10 seats). After those: Founding Partner 25% (Founding 100), then Founding Customer 20% (Founding 1,000). Do not give 30% away broadly.

### Partner assets

Partner Portal (`/partner`) for approved partners: dashboard, referral link, warm introduction, commission ledger, resources, illustrative calculator. Command Centre: `/command/partners`.

They **identify and introduce**; **Ben handles discovery, demonstration, and closing**.

## 90-day sales week (not “post and hope”)

**Every weekday:** personally initiate **10 customer** + **5 partner** conversations. Not 15 sales calls — 15 personal conversations started. Day themes below still apply (who you contact and what else you do). Copy: [FOUNDING-10-OUTREACH.md](./FOUNDING-10-OUTREACH.md).

Saturday and Sunday stay out of the engine except a short Sunday CEO review.

| Day | Focus | Blocks |
|-----|--------|--------|
| **Mon** | Existing network | 9:00–10:30 contact 10–15 people (book conversations). 10:30–12:00 follow-up. 1:00–3:00 demos / consultations. 3:00–4:00 proposals / Founding offers. 4:00–5:00 CRM — every contact has a next action. |
| **Tue** | Prospecting (RE first) | 9:00–11:00 new outbound. 11:00–12:00 follow-up. 1:00–3:00 demos. 3:00–4:00 **one useful piece** (e.g. “5 reasons agencies lose vendor opportunities after the enquiry”). 4:00–5:00 CRM + pipeline. |
| **Wed** | Partner day | 9:00–11:00 contact 10 potential Founding Resellers. 11:00–12:00 partner follow-ups. 1:00–3:00 partner demonstrations. 3:00–5:00 partner pipeline. |
| **Thu** | Authority | One substantial Insights piece (AI Search, visibility, systems, CRM, automation, RE lead gen, vendor acquisition). Distribute: website Insights, LinkedIn (company + personal), email, relevant contacts. Use existing IP: AI Visibility, Appraisal Magnet, Listing Pipeline, Vendor Velocity. |
| **Fri** | Closing | 9:00–11:00 every active prospect. 11:00–12:00 everyone who said “I’ll have a look.” 1:00–3:00 demos. 3:00–4:00 applications / proposals. 4:00–5:00 weekly numbers. |

**Sunday (30–60 min) — CEO review:** leads, consultations, proposals, applications, customers, partners, revenue, pipeline, biggest bottleneck. Pick **one** commercial constraint to attack that week.
