# Founding 10 — Outreach copy

**Status:** Locked · 18 August 2026  
**Use:** Existing network first. Personal update, not a SaaS campaign.  
**Engine:** [COMMERCIAL-ENGINE.md](./COMMERCIAL-ENGINE.md)

Tone: *I’ve been building something significant, it’s now ready, and I thought of you.*

Choose **customer** or **Founding Acquisition Partner** language before you send. Do not mix them in the first message.

Every conversation goes into the DigitalGate CRM with a **next action**.

**Daily baseline (weekdays):** initiate **10 customer** + **5 partner** conversations. Not 15 sales calls — 15 personal conversations started.

---

## Customer

### 1. Casual — SMS / WhatsApp

Hey mate, hope you’re well. I’ve been meaning to reach out because I’ve been working on something pretty significant over the past few years that I think could be really interesting to you.

I’ve built DigitalGate into an AI-powered Business Operating Platform that connects things like CRM, websites, AI, automation, communications, marketing and reporting into one system.

I’m now opening it up to the first 10 founding businesses in Australia, and I’m personally reaching out to people I’ve known over the years before I start broader outreach.

I’d love to show you what I’ve built and get your thoughts on whether it could be useful for your business.

Keen to have a chat sometime this week?

Ask for their perspective. Create curiosity. Do not ask for a sale.

### 6. If they reply “Yeah, tell me more”

Do not send a massive explanation.

Absolutely. The easiest way to explain it is that DigitalGate sits above the disconnected systems in a business and connects them into one operating platform.

So instead of having your website, CRM, marketing, communications, AI, automation and reporting all operating separately, they share the same business context.

I’ve also built specific Apps for things like Real Estate, Accommodation, SEO and AI Visibility.

Let me show you rather than try to explain it all over messages. When are you free for 20–30 minutes?

### 7. If they say “I’m interested but not sure what I’d use it for”

Buying signal. Reply:

That’s exactly why I’m doing the platform consultations.

I don’t want to just show you a bunch of features and tell you that you need them.

We’ll look at what you’re currently using, where things are disconnected or inefficient, and then work out whether DigitalGate actually makes sense for you.

If it doesn’t, I’ll tell you that too.

## Founding Acquisition Partner

You are not asking them to sell software. You are asking whether they want to help introduce DigitalGate into the market.

### 9. Casual

Hey mate, I’ve got another reason I wanted to reach out.

I’m putting together a very small group of people I trust who have strong relationships with business owners and a background in sales/business development.

I’m calling them DigitalGate Founding Acquisition Partners.

The idea isn’t for you to become a software salesperson. It’s more about identifying businesses in your network where DigitalGate could genuinely solve a problem and introducing them to me.

The founding programme includes a referral commission on customers you bring in, and the first cohort gets the highest level.

I immediately thought of you because of your network and experience.

Would you be interested in having a look at what I’m doing?

## Once they agree to a conversation

Do not immediately sell DigitalGate.

**First:** “Before I show you anything, tell me how you’re currently running the business digitally.”

Then:

- What CRM do you use?
- What do you use for your website?
- How do leads come in?
- Where do they go?
- How do you follow them up?
- What marketing systems are you using?
- How do you report on performance?
- What automation do you have?
- Where does customer information live?
- What frustrates you about the current setup?
- What are you paying for all of it?

Then show them **their** business through DigitalGate.
