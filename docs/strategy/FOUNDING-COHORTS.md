# DigitalGate Founding 10 — canonical cohort model

**Status:** Locked · September 2026

## Canonical decision

DigitalGate has one founding customer cohort only: **Founding 10**.

There is no Founding 100 or Founding 1,000 programme. Customer 11 onward is a standard DigitalGate customer using the published plans and normal self-service onboarding journey.

Founding status is programme/cohort status, not a pricing tier. Existing legitimate Founding 10 customers retain their explicitly promised benefits while eligible.

## Canonical customer journey

Standard customer:

```
Website → Start free 14-day trial → Onboarding → Platform
```

Founding 10:

```
Founding invitation → confirm Founding 10 terms → Onboarding → 14-day trial → Platform
```

Account/user and organisation provisioning happen as part of the onboarding journey. A separate enquiry, application, discovery call, emailed agreement, manual signature, manual acceptance or separate onboarding invitation must not gate self-service platform access.

A **Book a demo** route may remain available as an optional assisted-sales path.

## Terms acceptance

Founding 10 customers accept the applicable terms in-product. Acceptance must remain auditable and tied to the customer identity and organisation. Historical agreement/signature data may be retained for compatibility, but agreement-email and manual-signature workflow is not part of the canonical journey.

## Operator workflow

The customer-facing lifecycle is intentionally small:

```
Invited → Onboarding → Trial active → Live
```

Historical CRM/pipeline stages may remain in stored records for audit/history, but must not act as access gates.

## Founding cap

Founding 10 is scarce: no more than 10 qualifying customer organisations may receive programme status through the canonical Founding 10 programme. Enforcement must be authoritative server-side; UI counts alone are not sufficient.

## Pricing independence

Published platform pricing and canonical plan entitlements remain authoritative. Founding 10 does not create a parallel pricing catalogue.

## Architecture rule

Do not rebuild multi-cohort Founding infrastructure. Future campaigns, beta programmes, referral offers or promotions are separate commercial programmes and must not be labelled Founding 100/1,000.

The goal is a scalable self-service SaaS onboarding engine with a small Founding 10 status layered on top.
