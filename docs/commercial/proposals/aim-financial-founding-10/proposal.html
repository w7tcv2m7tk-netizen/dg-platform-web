Stage 1 · Executive summary
Executive summary

A connected digital foundation for AIM Financial and Hi-Vis Finance

DigitalGate will establish the technology and digital growth platform supporting both AIM Financial and Hi-Vis Finance — not as a simple website quotation, but as connected business infrastructure.

AIM Financial is the established business Nathan intends to retain. Hi-Vis Finance is being developed as a distinct digital business asset, with the option to grow and later separate or sell it.

The goal is to avoid fragmented tools and instead create one coherent system spanning website, CRM, leads, automation, AI, analytics, SEO and business data.

Website CRM Leads Automation AI Analytics SEO Business data
One platform. Two brands. One connected digital ecosystem.

What Stage 1 delivers

  • DigitalGate Platform implementation
  • AIM Financial website
  • Hi-Vis Finance website
  • CRM foundation & initial migration
  • Lead capture, automation & analytics
  • SEO & AI / Business Brain foundations
  • Architecture for later Hi-Vis separation

What Stage 1 deliberately excludes

  • Managed IT & cybersecurity (Stage 2)
  • Substantial bespoke third-party integrations until assessed and approved
  • Third-party licence fees unless explicitly stated
  • Paid advertising spend

Stage 2 Managed IT & Cybersecurity will be separately scoped later. No Stage 2 pricing is included here.

Stage 1 · Platform
DigitalGate Platform

The operating layer behind both brands

AIM and Hi-Vis will not just have websites sitting separately from the business systems. Website, CRM, automation and business data should work together.

Business Brain CRM Contacts Leads Opportunities Tasks Automation AI Analytics Website SEO Reporting Integrations Permissions Workflows Dashboards

Shared substrate

Identity, customers, context, permissions, events, intelligence, automation and reporting attach to one platform — so adding capability extends the system instead of creating another island.

Brand-aware structure

AIM and Hi-Vis keep distinct brand presence and attribution while sharing the DigitalGate operating layer during the initial engagement.

DigitalGate retains ownership of the platform, underlying technology, reusable modules and general IP. AIM retains ownership of its business data, content and client information.
