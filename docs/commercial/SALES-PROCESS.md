# Sales Process

**Status:** Founding 10 operating process · staff SSOT  
**Audience:** Ben + DigitalGate staff  
**Related:** [COMMERCIAL-ENGINE.md](../strategy/COMMERCIAL-ENGINE.md) · [FOUNDING-10-ACQUISITION.md](../strategy/FOUNDING-10-ACQUISITION.md) · [DISCOVERY-SCORING-SPEC.md](../strategy/DISCOVERY-SCORING-SPEC.md) · [FOUNDING-10-OUTREACH.md](../strategy/FOUNDING-10-OUTREACH.md)

DigitalGate **sells**. Resellers **introduce**. Qualification, demo, contract, onboarding, and billing stay with DigitalGate.

## Canonical path

```
INTRO / APPLICATION → DISCOVERY → CONSULTATION → ACCEPT / DECLINE → AGREEMENT → ONBOARDING
```

Two entries into the **same** Founding 10 cohort:

1. Public application — `digitalgate.com.au/founding-customers/`
2. Personal invitation — CRM **Invite to Founding 10** (invitation is not a seat)

`Founding 10: n / 10` counts opportunities at **accepted** or later only.

## Staff sequence

| Step | Owner | Where it lives |
|------|--------|----------------|
| Source | Network, RE prospecting, qualified reseller intro | CRM + Command Centre Sales Week |
| Score | Discovery scoring | Opportunity Score spec |
| Outreach | Personal update, not a SaaS blast | Outreach copy |
| Consult | Ben / DigitalGate | Calendar + CRM notes |
| Accept | Staff on the opportunity | Command Centre / CRM |
| Agreement | Customer signed-in | `/founding/agreement` |
| Configure | Customer + staff | Onboarding → Implementation → Go-live |

Scripts and questions: outreach doc. Seat math and loop: acquisition doc. Operating freeze (no website redesign while filling the 10): commercial engine.

## Do not

- Treat CRM as the product spec.
- Treat Platform Intelligence answers as a substitute for accept/decline.
- Let resellers demo, negotiate, or collect payment.
