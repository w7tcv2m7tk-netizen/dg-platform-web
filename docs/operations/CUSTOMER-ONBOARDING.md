## Business challenges discovery

Gen 2 onboarding already captures Goals & priorities and a Business Profile. Extend this deliberately rather than adding another disconnected questionnaire.

The onboarding discovery should ask, in plain business language:
1. What are the biggest challenges holding your business back right now?
2. Where does your team lose the most time?
3. Where do you think leads, revenue or opportunities are being lost?
4. What would you most like to improve over the next 6–12 months?
5. Which three issues matter most?

Persist the customer's wording as Business Brain context. Structured categories may be inferred alongside it for recommendations and analytics, but must not replace the original answer.

Use these answers to improve onboarding recommendations and implementation priorities. Do not automatically upsell an App simply because a challenge was selected; recommendations require a defensible challenge/evidence → solution fit.
