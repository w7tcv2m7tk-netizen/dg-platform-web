# Support

**Status:** Operating distinction · staff SSOT

| Channel | Use for |
|---------|---------|
| In-product success + Command Centre health | Adoption, Success Score, onboarding progress |
| Staff / customer support process | Break/fix, access, billing questions |
| Platform Intelligence | Questions about **how DigitalGate is designed** from allowlisted docs |
| Floating support chat | Tenant help — not staff architecture RAG |

Do not answer “what is our reseller commission?” from memory in chat. Cite [REFERRAL-AND-COMMISSION-RULES.md](../partners/REFERRAL-AND-COMMISSION-RULES.md) or the partner product.

Incident / security runbooks stay out of this catalogue until they are approved SOPs — see [INTERNAL-SOPS.md](./INTERNAL-SOPS.md).


## Business Challenge pathway

Support and Business Challenges are related but distinct:

| Need | Experience |
|---|---|
| Something is broken, inaccessible or billed incorrectly | Support |
| “How do I use DigitalGate?” | Product help / Support |
| “How do I solve this business problem?” | Business Challenge / Advisor / Success |
| Strategic optimisation and roadmap | Success plan / Business Challenge |

The customer-facing entry should use language such as **“I need help with a business challenge”** or **“What are you trying to solve?”**. The resulting challenge should be enriched with authorised Business Brain context and relevant Industry Intelligence before recommendations are made.

A recommendation may involve DigitalGate Apps, automation, workflow/process changes, marketing, website work, advisory action or a combination. It is acceptable for the correct recommendation to be outside DigitalGate; credibility takes precedence over forced product fit.

Challenge status and outcomes should eventually be measurable so the Business Brain learns what was tried and whether the business improved.
