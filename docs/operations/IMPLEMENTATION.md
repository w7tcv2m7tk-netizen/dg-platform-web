# Implementation

**Status:** Operating procedure · Founding 10  
**Product:** `/implementation` after onboarding

Implementation is **DigitalGate staff work** (and optional Professional Services). Resellers do not implement.

## Intent

Turn an accepted Founding customer into a configured organisation: Business Profile, connectors that matter for that industry, Core CRM truth, and the Industry / Growth Apps they actually bought.

## Do

- Follow the customer's implementation plan in product.
- Record blockers in CRM, not in a parallel spreadsheet-as-SSOT.
- Scope Professional Services separately from subscription.

## Do not

- Expand scope into unreleased Apps to close a deal.
- Skip go-live checks because onboarding checkboxes are green.
