# Founding Acquisition Partner Playbook

**Principle:** Acquisition Partners introduce and qualify. Ben closes. DigitalGate delivers.

Founding Acquisition Partner = **Introducer + Relationship Partner** — not merely an affiliate.

Partners see the full playbook at **`/partner/playbook`**. Staff Monday run-sheet: **`/command/partners/briefing`** (see also `FOUNDING-RESELLER-MEETING.md`).

## What we expect

1. **Identify** — use your network; recognise a good opportunity.
2. **Introduce** — warm intro beats handing over a name.
3. **Context** — who they are, what they do, why DigitalGate might fit.
4. **Relationship** — occasional reinforcement from a trusted contact.

## One sentence

> I've been working with Ben on a new platform called DigitalGate. It's designed to connect the different systems a business uses — CRM, website, marketing, AI, automation and more — into one operating platform. I've seen what they're building and thought it might be relevant to your business. Would you be open to having a conversation with him?

Then stop. Open the door — don’t sell the whole platform.

## Five principles

Connect · Centralise · Understand · Automate · Grow

## Journey

**IDENTIFY → INTRODUCE → DIGITALGATE CLOSES → DIGITALGATE DELIVERS → ACQUISITION PARTNER EARNS**

## Partner levels

- **Level 1 — Introducer:** warm introductions (start here)
- **Level 2 — DigitalGate Partner:** discuss proposition at business level; still no demo/config (aim here)
- **Level 3 — Strategic Acquisition Partner:** not required for first wave

## First 30 days

Week 1 understand + first intros → Week 2 continue + review objections → Week 3 refine targeting → Week 4 review results and evolve the partnership.

Quality over volume: one excellent introduction beats twenty poor leads.
