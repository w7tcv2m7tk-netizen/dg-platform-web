# Founding Reseller Playbook

**Principle:** Resellers introduce and qualify. Ben closes. DigitalGate delivers.

Founding Reseller = **Introducer + Relationship Partner** — not merely an affiliate.

## What we expect

1. **Identify potential businesses** — use your network; recognise a good opportunity, not every feature.
2. **Make the introduction** — warm intro beats handing over a name.
3. **Provide basic context** — who they are, what they do, why DigitalGate might fit, relationship strength.
4. **Help maintain the relationship** — occasional reinforcement from a trusted contact.

## One sentence

> I've been working with Ben, who's built a platform that connects the different systems a business uses — CRM, website, marketing, AI, automation and more — into one operating platform. I thought it might be worth you having a conversation with him.

## What resellers don't do

DigitalGate owns demos, configuration, onboarding, support, closing, pricing, and implementation.

## Journey

**Reseller:** Identify → initial conversation → qualify basic fit → warm introduction

**DigitalGate:** Discovery → consultation → demo → solution/pricing → Founding Customer application → acceptance → agreement → onboarding → implementation → go-live → ongoing relationship

**Reseller:** Commission → continue introducing

## Partner levels

- **Level 1 — Introducer:** warm introductions (start here)
- **Level 2 — DigitalGate Partner:** can discuss proposition at business level; still no demo/config (start here)
- **Level 3 — Strategic Reseller:** discovery, demos, industry specialisation — not for first wave

## Partner briefing (≈70 min)

1. Why DigitalGate exists (10 min)
2. What DigitalGate is (10 min)
3. Who it's for (10 min)
4. What makes a good prospect (10 min) — most important
5. How the process works (10 min)
6. How they make money (10 min)
7. Role play (10 min)
