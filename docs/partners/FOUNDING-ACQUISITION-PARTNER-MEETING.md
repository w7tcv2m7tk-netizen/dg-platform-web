# Founding Acquisition Partner Briefing

**Meeting:** Founding Acquisition Partner Briefing  
**Purpose:** Introduce the programme, establish the partner model, identify initial opportunities and agree how we work together.

**Surfaces**

- Staff run-sheet: `/command/partners/briefing`
- Partner playbook: `/partner/playbook`
- Constants: `packages/platform-core/src/partners/briefing.ts` + `programme.ts`

## Key message

> I'm not looking for you to become a software salesperson. I'm looking for people who already have relationships with business owners and can recognise where DigitalGate could genuinely help.

**Model:** You open doors → DigitalGate handles sales → DigitalGate delivers → You receive recurring commissions.

**Golden rule:** Acquisition Partners introduce. Ben closes. DigitalGate delivers.

## Agenda (≈90 min)

1. Welcome & why we’re here  
2. What is DigitalGate? (five principles: Connect · Centralise · Understand · Automate · Grow)  
3. Why DigitalGate needs acquisition partners (trust channel)  
4. What makes a good prospect + early markets  
5. Founding Acquisition Partner role (identify → introduce → context → relationship)  
6. What the acquisition partner does **not** need to do  
7. Sales process (12 stages)  
8. Journey line: IDENTIFY → INTRODUCE → DIGITALGATE CLOSES → DIGITALGATE DELIVERS → ACQUISITION PARTNER EARNS  
9. Partner levels 1–3 (start at 1/2)  
10. Introduction script + what not to say  
11. Commission & why attractive  
12. Founding status, weekly expectations, first 30 days  
13. Discussion prompts (network / opportunities / strengths / expectations)  
14. Close — Monday outcomes checklist  

## Monday outcomes

Clarity on: DigitalGate understanding, role, preferred industries, target network, first prospects, how introductions are made, support needed, commission understood, agreement/onboarding next steps, first intros identified, follow-up date.

**Goal:** Two motivated Founding Acquisition Partners who understand the model and are ready to open their first doors — not a perfect programme on day one.

## Closing line

> You bring the relationship. DigitalGate brings the platform. Together we create the opportunity.
