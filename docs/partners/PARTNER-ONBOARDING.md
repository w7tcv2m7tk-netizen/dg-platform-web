# Partner Onboarding

**Status:** Product workflow · staff SSOT  
**Surfaces:** `/partner` (invited partners) · `/command/partners` (staff)  
**Invite briefing:** [INVITE-BRIEFING.md](./INVITE-BRIEFING.md)

DigitalGate onboards the **introducer**. DigitalGate still onboards the **customer**.

## Sequence

1. Staff qualifies and invites (not self-serve affiliate signup).
2. Partner accepts programme rules at `/partner/terms` (recorded acceptance + version).
3. Partner uses Partner Dashboard, Referrals, Commissions, Demo.
4. Intros land in DigitalGate CRM; staff run sales process.
5. Commission accrues on qualifying **collected** Platform + App fees (Stripe `invoice.paid` → partner ledger when the referral is linked to the customer org).
6. Staff approve and pay commissions manually until Connect payouts exist.

## Demo account

Partners get a **demo org** to show what DigitalGate looks like — not to implement a customer, not to collect payment, not to replace Ben's consult.
