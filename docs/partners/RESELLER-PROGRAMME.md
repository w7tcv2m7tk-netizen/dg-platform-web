# Acquisition Partner Programme

**Programme name:** DigitalGate Founding Acquisition Partner Programme  
**Status:** Product + approved-copy lock · not binding legal terms until solicitor review  
**Code lock:** `packages/platform-core/src/partners/programme.ts`  
**Surface:** `/command/partners/acquisition`

This is a **controlled founding channel for trusted introducers**. It is **not** an affiliate programme, **not** automatic with Founding 10 membership, and **not** a traditional reseller who sells, owns, invoices or supports the customer.

## Positioning (locked)

> **Founding Acquisition Partner — Introduce. Refer. Earn recurring revenue.**

You introduce the opportunity. DigitalGate handles discovery, demonstration, proposal, onboarding and customer success.

Founding Acquisition Partners don’t sell, onboard or support customers. They introduce qualified businesses to DigitalGate. Ben / DigitalGate closes the opportunity.

## Operating model

```
Identify → Introduce → DigitalGate qualifies → DigitalGate closes → Customer goes live → Acquisition Partner earns recurring commission
```

| Step | Owner |
|------|--------|
| Introduce / refer | Founding Acquisition Partner |
| Qualify, demo, propose, close | DigitalGate |
| Implement / go-live | DigitalGate or Certified Delivery Partner |
| Earn recurring commission | Founding Acquisition Partner |

`Invitation → acceptance → referral → opportunity → customer → commission`

## What acquisition partners may do

Identify suitable Australian businesses, introduce decision-makers, share **approved** information, arrange an introduction, explain DigitalGate at a high level, and refer through the DigitalGate referral system.

## What acquisition partners must not do

Conduct technical demonstrations; configure, implement, or support DigitalGate; negotiate contracts or collect subscription payments; make product or performance guarantees; claim they are DigitalGate staff; own the customer relationship.
