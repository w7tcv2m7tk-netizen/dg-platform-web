# Reseller Programme

**Programme name:** DigitalGate Founding Reseller Programme  
**Status:** Product + approved-copy lock · not binding legal terms until solicitor review  
**Code lock:** `packages/platform-core/src/partners/programme.ts`

This is a **controlled founding channel for trusted introducers**. It is **not** an affiliate programme, **not** automatic with Founding 10 membership, and **not** a licence to sell, implement, or support DigitalGate.

## Operating model

> Reseller introduces → DigitalGate qualifies → Ben / DigitalGate demonstrates → Customer applies → DigitalGate accepts → Customer subscribes → Reseller earns commission

## Caps

| Rule | Value |
|------|--------|
| First wave (recruit excellent introducers) | 3–5 |
| Founding 10 reseller seat cap | 10 |

## What resellers may do

Identify suitable Australian businesses, introduce decision-makers, share **approved** information, arrange an introduction, explain DigitalGate at a high level, and refer through the DigitalGate referral system.

## What resellers must not do

Conduct technical demonstrations; configure, implement, or support DigitalGate; negotiate contracts or collect subscription payments; make product or performance guarantees; claim they are DigitalGate staff.
