# Reseller Terms

**Status:** Product surface exists · solicitor + accountant review required before treating as binding  
**Product:** `/partner/terms` (partners) · staff Partners nav

Programme rules in Platform Docs and `programme.ts` are **DigitalGate's commercial definitions** for product and approved copy. They are **not** published as binding legal terms until reviewed (GST, payment timing, attribution, agency).

## Staff rule

Do not invent a second terms document in chat, email, or CRM notes. Point partners at `/partner/terms` and this catalogue.

## What terms must eventually cover

Attribution, qualifying collected fees, 12-month commission window, exclusions (GST, refunds, services, pass-through), one reseller per customer, invitation/acceptance, and termination.

Until solicitor lock: use approved messaging only. See [REFERRAL-AND-COMMISSION-RULES.md](./REFERRAL-AND-COMMISSION-RULES.md).
