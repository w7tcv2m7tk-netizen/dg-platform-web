---

## Thesis

DigitalGate the **platform** is ahead of DigitalGate the **website**. The public site’s job is to accurately communicate the product that exists — not the old agency model.

**Central product thesis (every capability must strengthen this):**

> DigitalGate connects the business, understands the business, and helps the business act.

**Do not** add random features or SEO pages because there is room. New capability and new content must reinforce the operating-system story.

---

## Public site mirrors the product

```
DIGITALGATE
Business Operating Platform

CORE
  Business · CRM · Communications · Documents · Commerce ·
  Design Studio · Infrastructure · Intelligence

INDUSTRY
  Property · Hospitality · Services · Finance · Professional ·
  Health · Automotive · Retail · Creator & Media · …

GROWTH
  Prospecting · AI Visibility · SEO · Automation · Analytics ·
  Social · Reputation · Marketing · …

INTELLECTUAL LAYER (over the OS)
  Digital Twin™ → Business Brain™ → AI Advisor → Action → Learning
```

Communications and Documents & Signing are **OS pieces**, not bolt-on marketing features.

Professional Services (implementation, websites, SEO delivery, ads support) sit **under** the Platform — never as the headline product.

---

## Content architecture rule

**No SEO content farms.** Every new public page needs a place in the knowledge chain:

```
Insight → Concept → Product → Industry application → Audit/CTA
```

Example:

```
What is AI Visibility?
  → AI Visibility Framework™
  → AI Visibility for Real Estate
  → AI Visibility App
  → Agency Growth Audit
  → DigitalGate Platform
```

Reject pages that only exist to rank for a keyword without linking Insight → Concept → Product → Industry → CTA.

## Clarify (Priority 2) — acceptance criteria

When Clarify is done, a first-time visitor can answer without scrolling deep:

1. What is DigitalGate? → Business Operating Platform  
2. How is it structured? → Core / Industry / Growth (+ Infrastructure as needed)  
3. What sits on top? → Twin → Brain → Advisor → Action  
4. What do I buy? → Platform tiers + Apps (not agency retainers)

Primary surfaces: homepage architecture block, `/apps` hub, nav labels, pricing hierarchy, About one-liner.

---

## Prove (Priority 3) — acceptance criteria

- Interface evidence for Twin / Brain / key Apps (screens, not stock)  
- Honest case narratives: Roe Realty, Currumbin Valley Hideaway (what was connected, what changed)  
- No fabricated revenue claims

---

## Convert (Priority 4) — acceptance criteria

- Audit produces a visible **Growth Score** (or equivalent)  
- Path: Audit → Score → Diagnosis → Roadmap → Platform  
- Primary RE commercial funnel; secondary general business audit
