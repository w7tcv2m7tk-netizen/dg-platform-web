## Thesis

DigitalGate the **platform** is ahead of DigitalGate the **website**. The public site’s job is to accurately communicate the product that exists — not the old agency model.

When AI/search engines omit DigitalGate from “intelligent platform for running, understanding and growing a real estate business,” that is primarily an **entity authority / discoverability** gap. Product thesis can be right while external mentions lag. The website + content architecture must close that gap.

**Central product thesis (every capability must strengthen this):**

> DigitalGate connects the business, understands the business, and helps the business act.

**Category language** (see [CATEGORY-POSITIONING.md](./CATEGORY-POSITIONING.md)):

> DigitalGate is an **intelligent Business Operating Platform**.  
> Connect your business. Build its Digital Twin™. Give it a Business Brain™.  
> **Run. Understand. Grow.**

**Do not** add random features or SEO pages because there is room. New capability and new content must reinforce the operating-system story. CRM is one part of the system — not the whole product.

## Content architecture rule

**No SEO content farms.** Every new public page needs a place in the knowledge chain:

```
Insight → Concept → Product → Industry application → Audit/CTA
```

Example (AI Visibility):

```
What is AI Visibility?
  → AI Visibility Framework™
  → AI Visibility for Real Estate
  → AI Visibility App
  → Agency Growth Audit
  → DigitalGate Platform
```

Reject pages that only exist to rank for a keyword without that chain.

## Clarify (Priority 2) — acceptance criteria

Copy and IA must follow [CATEGORY-POSITIONING.md](./CATEGORY-POSITIONING.md).

A first-time visitor can answer without scrolling deep:

1. What is DigitalGate? → **Intelligent Business Operating Platform**  
2. How does it work? → Connect → Twin → Brain; **Run. Understand. Grow.**  
3. How is it structured? → Core / Industry / Growth + Intelligence layer  
4. How is it different from Rex/CRM? → Runs the **business**; RE is one environment — not “better Rex”  
5. What do I buy? → Platform tiers + Apps (not agency retainers)

Primary surfaces: homepage hero/architecture, `/apps` hub, nav, pricing hierarchy, About, Organization schema description.

---

## Prove (Priority 3) — acceptance criteria

- Interface evidence for Twin / Brain / key Apps (screens, not stock)  
- Honest case narratives: Roe Realty, Currumbin Valley Hideaway

- No fabricated revenue claims
