DigitalGate processes this information primarily to provide the Platform and services requested by the customer.

Where appropriate, DigitalGate may act as a service provider or processor on behalf of the customer.

Customer-controlled business data (for example contacts, leads, documents and connected system records within a customer organisation) is distinct from DigitalGate account, telemetry, billing, security and product data that we collect to operate and improve the Platform.

Customer-controlled business data remains subject to the customer’s instructions and applicable law. DigitalGate’s own operational data is handled under this Privacy Policy for DigitalGate’s legitimate business purposes described above.

Organisations, workspaces and permissions

A subscriber may operate multiple DigitalGate Organisations where their plan permits it. Each business or Organisation is treated as a distinct tenant/workspace. Business data, CRM records, Digital Twin information, Aida context, documents, connector data and integration credentials are organisation-scoped. Access to an individual Organisation is permission-controlled. A user may be authorised to access multiple Organisations. Information is not automatically shared between separate Organisations merely because they belong to the same subscription or account.
