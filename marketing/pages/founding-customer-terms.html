Referral status is described as ongoing while you remain an eligible DigitalGate customer / referral partner. DigitalGate does not promise irrevocable “forever” commission rights. Referral commission applies for the first 12 months of each referred customer’s qualifying subscription only. There is no indefinite or lifetime commission liability on referred subscriptions beyond that period.
