Founding 10 Customer Terms

These terms govern participation in DigitalGate's single invitation-only founding customer cohort, Founding 10. They operate alongside DigitalGate's general Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, published pricing and any organisation-specific written commercial offer.

Programme lock: DigitalGate has one founding cohort only — Founding 10. There is no Founding 100 or Founding 1,000 programme. New customers after the ten qualifying Founding organisations join DigitalGate on standard commercial terms.

1. Eligibility and invitation

  • Founding 10 participation is by DigitalGate invitation only.
  • No more than ten qualifying customer organisations may hold canonical Founding 10 programme status.
  • An invitation is not transferable and does not create programme rights until the applicable terms are accepted.
  • DigitalGate may withdraw an unaccepted invitation or decline participation where eligibility, capacity, fraud, abuse or operational concerns reasonably require it.

2. Customer journey and acceptance

The canonical Founding journey is invitation → Founding terms acceptance → onboarding → 14-day trial → platform. Terms acceptance may occur electronically in-product and may be recorded with the accepting user's identity, organisation, date, time and applicable terms version.

A separate enquiry, discovery call, emailed agreement, manual signature or separate onboarding invitation is not required unless DigitalGate and the customer expressly agree to an assisted commercial process.

3. Pricing and billing

  • Founding status is a programme benefit, not a separate pricing tier.
  • Founding customers pay DigitalGate's standard published Platform, App, Template, Success and usage pricing unless a written organisation-specific commercial offer expressly overrides it.
  • The standard trial is 14 days where offered under DigitalGate's current commercial configuration.
  • Industry Apps are currently $99/month each and include one primary Industry Template; additional Templates are currently $29/month each, subject to the published pricing terms in force from time to time.
  • Specialist Industry integrations and API access remain subject to the customer's Platform plan, activated Industry App/Template, provider availability and third-party terms or charges.
  • Managing multiple businesses under one subscription account remains subject to the Platform plan entitlements and limits published by DigitalGate.

4. Founding programme benefits

While eligible, a Founding 10 customer may receive benefits such as priority onboarding, closer founder access, selected early product access, roadmap feedback opportunities and optional founding recognition. These benefits do not guarantee delivery dates, specific roadmap decisions, uninterrupted beta access or permanent feature availability.

5. Founding referral commission

Eligible Founding 10 customers may receive a 20% direct referral commission for the first 12 months of a referred customer's qualifying paid subscription fees actually received by DigitalGate.

  • Qualifying fees are recurring Platform, Industry App, Industry Template and other expressly eligible subscription fees actually received.
  • Taxes, refunds, chargebacks, credits, failed payments, one-off professional services, pass-through charges and the referrer's own account are excluded unless DigitalGate expressly states otherwise.
  • Referral commission is single-level. Founding status does not authorise resale, representation, contracting or price-setting on behalf of DigitalGate.
  • The separate Acquisition Partner programme, where offered, is a different programme with separate eligibility and terms.

6. Programme conduct and status

Customers must use the programme and platform lawfully and in good faith. DigitalGate may suspend or end Founding benefits for fraud, abuse, material breach, misuse of referral arrangements or conduct that creates material security, legal or operational risk. Ending Founding programme status does not erase amounts already properly accrued under applicable referral terms.

7. Platform entitlements and third parties

Founding status does not override technical or commercial entitlements. Platform tiers, Industry Apps, Templates, specialist APIs, multi-business limits, communications, AI usage, storage and third-party connectors remain governed by the applicable subscription, published limits, provider availability and reasonable fair-use controls.

8. Changes

DigitalGate may update these programme terms prospectively. Material changes affecting an existing participant will be handled consistently with applicable law, the general Terms of Service and any binding organisation-specific offer. DigitalGate will not create additional cohorts labelled Founding 100 or Founding 1,000 under this programme.

9. General terms

DigitalGate's general Terms of Service and Privacy Policy continue to apply. If an organisation-specific written offer expressly conflicts with these programme terms on a commercial entitlement, the specific written offer controls to the extent of that conflict.

10. Contact
