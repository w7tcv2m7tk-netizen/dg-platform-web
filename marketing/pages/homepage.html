Plain meanings

What the words actually mean.

Clear definitions — not a feature catalogue.

DigitalGate
An AI-powered Business Operating Platform that connects customer data, operations, digital presence, intelligence and automation into one shared business context.
Digital Twin
The living digital representation of the business — a continuously updated picture of what is happening across customers, opportunities, presence, revenue and operations.
Business Brain
The intelligence layer that turns a connected business into a smart business — combining Business DNA, Twin state, knowledge and goals so DigitalGate understands what the business is and what it needs next.
AI Advisor
The reasoning surface that turns shared context into clear priorities, explanations and recommended actions. Separate from the Business Brain, which holds understanding.
AI Visibility
How the business is understood and discoverable in AI-era search and recommendation systems — readiness to be found, understood and recommended, measured honestly from real presence signals.
