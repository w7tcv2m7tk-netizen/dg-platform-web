Founding referral benefit

20% referral commission for 12 months.

Eligible Founding 10 customers may receive a 20% direct referral commission on qualifying subscription fees actually received from a referred customer during that customer's first 12 months.

  • Separate from the referrer's own subscription pricing.
  • Applies only to qualifying recurring fees actually received.
  • No multi-level structure and no resale authority.
  • Participation remains subject to the Founding Customer Terms.

Not a second price list

Founding 10 does not include an automatic recurring discount and does not unlock every Industry App, specialist integration or third-party service. Normal plan entitlements continue to apply.
