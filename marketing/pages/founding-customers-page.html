Commercial Model

Founding commercial model — access, not a % discount

Earlier cohorts receive the greatest founding programme advantage. Subscription pricing is the published rate for everyone.

01

Published pricing

Founding customers pay standard published Platform + Apps pricing. Try DigitalGate free for 14 days. Choose monthly or annual billing — annual ≈ 10 months of monthly pricing (save ~2 months).

02

Programme benefits

Limited places, priority onboarding, early access to selected Apps, founder relationship and roadmap influence. Founding 10 is limited access — not discounted access.

03

What is not included

Founding benefits do not automatically include Professional Services (including website builds), Customer Success / Support plans, custom development, consulting, migration or implementation — unless expressly included in your Founding offer.

04

Founding Referral Status

Founding customers receive a dedicated direct referral tier and can earn referral commission on qualifying subscriptions from direct referrals for the first 12 months of each referred customer's subscription — completely separate from your own subscription. Direct referral rates by cohort: Founding 10 — 20% · Founding 100 — 15% · Founding 1,000+ — 10%. The separate Acquisition Partner programme (invitation only, 25%) is not the same as these direct customer referral rates. Referral status does not authorise you to bind DigitalGate, set pricing or represent yourself as an authorised agent unless separately agreed in writing.

Founding benefits do not include unlimited support, unlimited custom development, lifetime free Apps or services, guaranteed roadmap delivery, or a recurring percentage discount on Platform + Apps. Programme rules: Founding Customer Terms & Conditions.
